Pricing

Last updated: 8 September 2026

ReelSpark (operated by Matrigyan Private Limited) charges a single annual registration fee. There is no automatic recurring billing — each year's renewal is a payment you choose to make. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹).

1. Annual registration fee

What it isA fee that unlocks the ability to submit your YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels for cross-promotion in the ReelSpark feed.
Amount₹300 per year (inclusive of GST — see section 3).
Access period12 months from the date the payment is confirmed.
RenewalNot automatic. Make a fresh annual payment to keep submitting after the period ends.
What's includedAccount access to browse the feed, submit videos (subject to fair-use / rate limits and moderation), see in-app view counts on your submissions, and participate in the referral programme.
What's not includedAny guarantee of views, watch time, followers, revenue or monetisation on YouTube / Instagram.
RefundsNon-refundable once access is enabled, except for payment errors — see the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

2. Referral bonus

Bonus per successful referral₹50, credited to your in-app referral balance when a user you invited completes paid registration.
Minimum withdrawal₹150 of accumulated, validated referral balance.
Payout methodUPI transfer to the UPI ID you provide, paid within 2 working days of an approved request.
ConditionsReferral balance is a promotional credit, not a cash deposit or stored-value instrument. Credits from self-referral, fake accounts or other abuse are void. See Terms, section 5.

3. Taxes

Matrigyan Private Limited is registered under GST. GSTIN: 19AAQCM7780C1ZY (West Bengal). The ₹300 annual registration fee shown at checkout is inclusive of GST — it is the final amount payable, with no tax added on top. A GST invoice / payment receipt is available on request at support@reelspark.in.

4. Accepted payment methods

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. Depending on availability you can pay via UPI, debit card, credit card, net banking or supported wallets. ReelSpark does not store your card or bank credentials — see the Privacy Policy.

5. Changes to pricing

We may revise pricing prospectively. Any change is shown in the app before you pay and does not affect an access period you have already paid for.

6. Refunds

The annual registration fee is non-refundable once access is enabled, apart from genuine payment errors. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for details and how to raise a claim.
