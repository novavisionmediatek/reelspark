Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

ReelSpark (operated by Matrigyan Private Limited) offers one paid product: an annual registration fee that unlocks the ability to submit your YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels for cross-promotion for a period of twelve (12) months. This page explains our cancellation and refund position. Read it together with our Terms & Conditions.

1. Nature of the purchase

The annual registration fee buys immediate digital access to a paid feature of the app for a 12-month period. There is no physical product and nothing is shipped. Once your payment is confirmed and submission access is enabled on your account, the service has been delivered in full for that access period.

2. Cancellation

  • You can stop using ReelSpark and delete your account at any time from the in-app account settings or by emailing support@reelspark.in.
  • The annual registration fee does not renew automatically — there is no recurring charge to cancel. Each year's renewal is a payment you choose to make.
  • Cancelling or deleting your account during an active access period does not entitle you to a refund of the fee already paid.

3. The annual registration fee is non-refundable

Once your registration is approved and submission access is enabled, the annual registration fee is non-refundable and no pro-rata or partial refund is given for the unused part of the access period. This includes (without limitation):

  • you changed your mind, or no longer wish to use the app;
  • you did not receive the number of views, watch time, followers or engagement you hoped for — ReelSpark does not guarantee results (see Terms, section 11);
  • you did not use the service during the access period;
  • your account was suspended or terminated for breaching the Terms (e.g. fake engagement, submitting content you don't own, abuse of the referral programme);
  • your third-party YouTube / Instagram account was actioned by those platforms.

4. Limited exceptions (payment errors)

The only situations in which money is returned are genuine payment or provisioning errors:

  • Duplicate / double charge — you were charged more than once for the same registration. The extra amount(s) are returned in full.
  • Amount debited but payment failed — money left your account but the transaction failed at Razorpay's end and access was not granted. Such amounts are normally auto-reversed by your bank within 5–7 working days; if not, contact us and we will assist.
  • Payment captured but access never enabled — your payment succeeded but, due to a technical fault on our side, submission access was not enabled within 72 hours and we are unable to fix it. We will enable access or, failing that, refund the fee.
  • Unauthorised transaction — a payment made without your authorisation, subject to verification and any investigation by Razorpay or your bank.

5. Referral balance & withdrawals

  • Referral bonus credits are promotional and are not purchased by you, so they are not "refundable". They can be withdrawn only per the rules on the Pricing page (minimum threshold, valid payout UPI ID, verification).
  • Approved withdrawal requests are paid to your UPI ID within 2 working days.
  • Credits earned through fraud, self-referral or fake accounts may be reversed and are not payable.

6. How to raise a payment-error claim

  1. Email support@reelspark.in from your registered email address within 7 days of the charge.
  2. Include: your registered name and email, the payment date and amount, and the payment reference / UPI UTR / Razorpay payment ID (and a screenshot if available).
  3. We acknowledge within 2 business days and decide within 7 business days, subject to any bank / payment-partner investigation.

7. How approved returns are paid

Where a return is approved under section 4, it is issued to the original payment method through Razorpay. Once initiated, it typically takes 5–7 business days to reflect, depending on your bank or card issuer. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR); we are not responsible for third-party bank charges.
