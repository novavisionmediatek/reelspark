Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

ReelSpark (operated by Matrigyan Private Limited) is a fully digital service. No physical goods are sold, and nothing is shipped. This policy describes how the paid digital service is delivered.

1. What you receive

Payment of the annual registration fee unlocks video submission access inside your existing ReelSpark account for a period of twelve (12) months. There is no shipment, courier, tracking number or delivery address involved.

2. Delivery method & timeline

Delivery channelElectronically, within the ReelSpark app / website at https://reelspark.in, on the account used to make the payment.
Standard timelineAccess is enabled automatically immediately after the payment is confirmed by Razorpay (typically within a few seconds to a few minutes).
Manually verified paymentsWhere a payment is verified manually (UPI reference + screenshot), access is enabled after review, normally within 24 hours.
Access period12 months from the date the payment is confirmed. Renew with a fresh annual payment to continue after that.
ConfirmationThe app updates your registration status; a confirmation may also be sent to your registered email.

3. Delivery charges

There are no shipping, handling or delivery charges. The only amount payable is the annual registration fee shown on the Pricing page, and that amount is inclusive of GST (GSTIN 19AAQCM7780C1ZY).

4. If access is not delivered

If your payment succeeded but submission access is not enabled within the timelines above, contact support@reelspark.in with your payment reference. We will enable access or, if we cannot, return the fee under the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

5. Service availability

The service is delivered over the internet and depends on your device, your network, and the availability of third-party platforms (YouTube, Instagram) and infrastructure providers. Occasional maintenance or downtime may temporarily affect access.
