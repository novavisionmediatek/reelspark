4. Legal bases

We process data on the basis of your consent, the performance of our contract with you (the Terms), our legitimate interests in running and securing the Platform, and compliance with law. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 8); this does not affect processing already carried out.

5. Payment processing

Online payments are handled by Razorpay Software Private Limited, a PCI-DSS compliant payment aggregator. When you pay, your card / UPI / bank details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay under its own privacy policy and terms. Matrigyan Private Limited receives only the payment outcome and a reference identifier. We recommend you review Razorpay's privacy policy at razorpay.com/privacy.

6. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and file-storage provider, which also sends the automated account-confirmation and password-reset emails;
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery for the app and website;
  • Razorpay — payment processing and payment-fraud checks (see section 5);
  • YouTube and Instagram — when their embeds and public APIs are used to render and fetch metadata for submitted videos (subject to their own privacy policies);
  • Legal & safety — authorities, regulators or courts where required by law, or to protect our rights, our users, or the public;
  • Business transfers — a successor entity in a merger, acquisition or reorganisation, subject to this Policy.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Cookies & local storage

We use only essential browser local storage / session storage — to keep you signed in and to carry a referral code from an invite link into sign-up. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. Blocking this storage may break sign-in and other features.

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • access a copy of your personal data and request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your account and associated personal data;
  • withdraw consent and object to or restrict certain processing;
  • request data portability where applicable; and
  • nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any right, email support@reelspark.in from your registered email, or use the in-app account settings. We may need to verify your identity and may retain limited data as required by law (e.g. transaction records).

9. Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform. After account deletion we delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period, except records we must retain for legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution or fraud-prevention purposes (financial records are typically retained for up to 8 years under Indian law).

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — encryption in transit, access controls, row-level security on our database, and private storage buckets for uploaded payment proofs. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; you use the Platform at your own risk and should keep your credentials confidential.

11. Children

The Platform is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our providers (Supabase, Cloudflare, Razorpay) may process data on servers outside India. Where they do, we rely on contractual and technical safeguards consistent with applicable law.
